📲 How to Install on Apple iPhone

Apple Safari requires installing apps through the Share menu:

Step 1:

Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square box with an arrow pointing up: ⎋).

Step 2:

Scroll down the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen" (with the ➕ icon).

Step 3:

Tap "Add" in the top-right corner. The app icon will appear on your iPhone home screen!

Once added, open the icon on your home screen to run in full-screen app mode.
